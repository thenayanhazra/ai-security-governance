# AI risk report to the board: template

A quarterly report designed to be read in five minutes. It answers four questions: are we within appetite, what changed, what is coming, and what do you need to decide. Replace everything in square brackets. A [completed example](example-board-report-q3-2026.md) shows the level of detail intended.

Keep it to two pages. Detail belongs in an appendix or with the AI risk forum.

---

[Three sentences. Overall position against appetite; the single most important change since last quarter; the decision requested, if any.]
